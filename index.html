01 / Method

What happens after you search

The first source is broad place data. Specialist registries and web checks are separate adapters, so a healthcare license is never confused with a restaurant listing or a live website.

A missing field is not proof that the information does not exist. The product records what the source supplied, what another check found and what still needs review.

01

Find candidates

Filter a bounded public dataset by taxonomy and geography.

02

Normalize and deduplicate

Keep source IDs while resolving repeated names, phones and addresses conservatively.

03

Attach evidence

Return provenance, license, timestamp and uncertainty with every accepted record.
