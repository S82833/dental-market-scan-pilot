01 / The deliverable

What you're actually buying

The first pilot is assembled and checked by hand. You receive 25 active dental practices, the reason each record was selected, and the evidence used in the review.

We do not claim that a practice has no website. We report what we found, what we did not find after two searches, and which records still need another pass.

01

Find an active practice

Start with a current public registry record, not a scraped list with unknown provenance.

02

Search twice

Check the legal name with both phone and address. Directory pages do not count as an official site.

03

Keep the uncertainty

A weak match stays uncertain. A confirmed domain is excluded. The list only contains records worth a closer look.
